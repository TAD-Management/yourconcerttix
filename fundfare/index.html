Good to know

Questions

Where does the $2 go?

To the school. $2 is paid for each completed ticket purchase made through the school’s link, never to an individual.

Can each team have its own link?

Yes. Use one school link, or give each team, camp and club its own. Each link keeps its own total, so softball, band camp and the drama club can each track their goal.

Who can share the link?

Students, teachers and families. Younger students share through a parent or teacher.

Which concerts count?

TAD Entertainment concerts at the Apache Junction Performing Arts Center (AJPAC). There are 30+ shows in the series.

Who runs the ticketing?

Tickets are sold and delivered by FanGenie. Your school’s link simply tells FanGenie which school or team to credit.
